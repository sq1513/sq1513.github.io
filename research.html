Does Rural E-Commerce Reduce Street Crime? Evidence From Taobao Villages in China

Yunze Han, Qing Sun, and Kei Kajisa

Bulletin of Economic Research, 2026

We use county-level data from China to study how the emergence of Taobao villages relates to property crime. Counties that gained a Taobao village saw a decline in property-related street and neighborhood crime.

The effect of government expenditure and comparative advantage on agricultural green development in OECD countries

Qing Sun and Shinichi Kitano

Journal of Environmental Information Science, 2026(1), 25–36

We compare conventional and green measures of agricultural productivity across OECD countries from 2005 to 2021. The association with government spending is smaller when the measure accounts for carbon emissions and soil carbon sequestration.
